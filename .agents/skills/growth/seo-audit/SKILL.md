# Seo Audit

## Purpose

Audit SEO across technical, content, and search-intent dimensions.

## Procedure

1. Review crawl/index basics.
2. Review templates/metadata/internal links.
3. Map intent/content gaps.
4. Prioritize by expected impact and effort.
5. Define measurement.

## Guardrails

- Do not manufacture keyword volume without data.

## Output

Return:
- result
- evidence
- risks
- required follow-up

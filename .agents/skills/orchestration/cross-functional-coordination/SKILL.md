# Cross Functional Coordination

## Purpose
Coordinate work spanning multiple startup or engineering domains without losing ownership or decision clarity.

## Procedure
1. Define the shared outcome.
2. Identify domain owners and dependencies.
3. Separate sequential from parallel work.
4. Define handoff evidence.
5. Identify decision owner for disagreements.
6. Track blockers and human approval boundaries.
7. Consolidate the final result.

## Guardrails
- Do not create meetings or agent involvement without a concrete dependency.
- One owner remains accountable for the integrated outcome.

## Output
Return result, ownership map, dependencies, risks, and handoff state.

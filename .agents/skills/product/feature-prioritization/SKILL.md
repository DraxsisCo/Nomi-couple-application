# Feature Prioritization

## Purpose

Prioritize product work using value, evidence, cost, and risk.

## Procedure

1. Clarify outcome.
2. Estimate evidence strength.
3. Estimate cost/risk/dependencies.
4. Compare simpler alternatives.
5. Recommend build/experiment/defer/reject.

## Guardrails

- Do not let sunk engineering effort determine value.

## Output

Return:
- result
- evidence
- risks
- required follow-up

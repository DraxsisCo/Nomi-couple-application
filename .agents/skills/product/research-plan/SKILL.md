# Research Plan

## Purpose

Create a focused user/product research plan.

## Procedure

1. State question.
2. Select participants/evidence.
3. Choose method.
4. Define prompts/tasks.
5. Define synthesis approach and decision linkage.

## Guardrails

- Research is not a ritual; tie it to a decision.

## Output

Return:
- result
- evidence
- risks
- required follow-up

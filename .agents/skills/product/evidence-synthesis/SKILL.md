# Evidence Synthesis

## Purpose

Synthesize qualitative/quantitative product evidence.

## Procedure

1. Separate observation from interpretation.
2. Cluster recurring patterns.
3. Track contradictions.
4. Assess evidence strength.
5. Recommend next decision or experiment.

## Guardrails

- Do not convert anecdotes into fake percentages.

## Output

Return:
- result
- evidence
- risks
- required follow-up

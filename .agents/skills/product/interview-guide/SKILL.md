# Interview Guide

## Purpose

Create a non-leading interview guide.

## Procedure

1. Start from recent concrete behavior.
2. Ask open questions.
3. Probe alternatives and costs.
4. Avoid pitching.
5. Capture evidence and uncertainty.

## Guardrails

- Do not ask users to predict imaginary future behavior as primary evidence.

## Output

Return:
- result
- evidence
- risks
- required follow-up

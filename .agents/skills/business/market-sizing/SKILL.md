# Market Sizing

## Purpose

Estimate TAM/SAM/SOM using explicit assumptions.

## Procedure

1. Choose top-down and/or bottom-up method.
2. Define units.
3. State assumptions.
4. Triangulate.
5. Provide ranges and sensitivity.

## Guardrails

- Precision beyond evidence is theater.

## Output

Return:
- result
- evidence
- risks
- required follow-up

# Pre Mortem

## Purpose

Assume the initiative failed and identify plausible causes.

## Procedure

1. Define failure horizon.
2. Generate plausible causes across product/tech/market/ops.
3. Prioritize controllable risks.
4. Create preventative signals/actions.

## Guardrails

- Keep causes specific enough to monitor.

## Output

Return:
- result
- evidence
- risks
- required follow-up

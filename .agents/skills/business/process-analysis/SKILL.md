# Process Analysis

## Purpose

Map and improve an operational business process.

## Procedure

1. Identify actors.
2. Map trigger-to-outcome.
3. Identify handoffs and waste.
4. Identify controls.
5. Recommend simpler future state.

## Guardrails

- Automating a broken process makes a faster broken process.

## Output

Return:
- result
- evidence
- risks
- required follow-up

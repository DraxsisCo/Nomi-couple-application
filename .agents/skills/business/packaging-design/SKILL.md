# Packaging Design

## Purpose

Design product tiers/packaging.

## Procedure

1. Identify natural segmentation.
2. Choose value metric.
3. Define tier boundaries.
4. Avoid feature hostage-taking that harms adoption.
5. Check upgrade path.

## Guardrails

- Keep packaging understandable.

## Output

Return:
- result
- evidence
- risks
- required follow-up

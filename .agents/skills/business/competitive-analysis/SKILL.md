# Competitive Analysis

## Purpose

Compare relevant competitors and substitutes.

## Procedure

1. Define evaluation dimensions.
2. Include status quo/manual alternatives.
3. Gather evidence.
4. Separate fact from inference.
5. Identify positioning implications.

## Guardrails

- Do not produce feature-count bingo as strategy.

## Output

Return:
- result
- evidence
- risks
- required follow-up

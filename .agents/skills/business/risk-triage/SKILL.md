# Risk Triage

## Purpose

Classify risk by severity, likelihood, detectability, and reversibility.

## Procedure

1. Identify risk event.
2. Assess impact.
3. Assess likelihood.
4. Assess detection/recovery.
5. Assign owner/mitigation/escalation.

## Guardrails

- Use evidence where possible.

## Output

Return:
- result
- evidence
- risks
- required follow-up

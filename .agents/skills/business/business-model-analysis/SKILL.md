# Business Model Analysis

## Purpose

Analyze how the product creates, delivers, and captures value.

## Procedure

1. Identify customer and job.
2. Map value proposition.
3. Map revenue/cost drivers.
4. Identify dependencies.
5. Stress-test assumptions.

## Guardrails

- Separate current evidence from hypotheses.

## Output

Return:
- result
- evidence
- risks
- required follow-up

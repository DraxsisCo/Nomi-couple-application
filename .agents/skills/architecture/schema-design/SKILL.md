# Schema Design

## Purpose

Design or revise persistent data structures.

## Procedure

1. Identify entities and invariants.
2. Choose constraints and relationships.
3. Define nullability/default semantics.
4. Consider access patterns.
5. Plan migration/backfill.
6. Review retention and sensitive data.

## Guardrails

- Do not optimize by denormalizing without a measured reason.

## Output

Return:
- result
- evidence
- risks
- required follow-up

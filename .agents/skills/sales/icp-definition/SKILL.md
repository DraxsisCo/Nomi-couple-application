# Icp Definition

## Purpose

Define an ideal customer profile grounded in pain, budget, and reachability.

## Procedure

1. Define company/user context.
2. Identify urgent pain.
3. Identify buying trigger.
4. Identify disqualifiers.
5. Map reachable signals.

## Guardrails

- ICP is not 'everyone who might benefit'.

## Output

Return:
- result
- evidence
- risks
- required follow-up

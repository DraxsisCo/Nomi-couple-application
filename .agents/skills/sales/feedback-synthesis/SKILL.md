# Feedback Synthesis

## Purpose

Turn customer feedback into structured product evidence.

## Procedure

1. Separate request from underlying problem.
2. Cluster patterns.
3. Weight by segment/context.
4. Link to existing product evidence.
5. Recommend action or research.

## Guardrails

- Loud customers are not automatically representative.

## Output

Return:
- result
- evidence
- risks
- required follow-up

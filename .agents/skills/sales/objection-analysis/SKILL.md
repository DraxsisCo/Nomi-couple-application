# Objection Analysis

## Purpose

Analyze objections and prepare evidence-based responses.

## Procedure

1. Classify objection.
2. Identify underlying risk.
3. Respond with evidence/options.
4. Capture product feedback.
5. Avoid overpromising.

## Guardrails

- Never invent customers, metrics, or capabilities.

## Output

Return:
- result
- evidence
- risks
- required follow-up

# Qualification

## Purpose

Assess whether an opportunity deserves sales effort.

## Procedure

1. Check problem urgency.
2. Check authority/process.
3. Check fit and constraints.
4. Check timing.
5. Record disqualifiers and next evidence needed.

## Guardrails

- Disqualification is productive.

## Output

Return:
- result
- evidence
- risks
- required follow-up

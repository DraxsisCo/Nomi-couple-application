# Account Research

## Purpose

Research an account using approved available sources.

## Procedure

1. Confirm target criteria.
2. Gather public/company evidence.
3. Identify likely use case and trigger.
4. Record source and uncertainty.
5. Prepare concise outreach context.

## Guardrails

- Do not fabricate personal facts.

## Output

Return:
- result
- evidence
- risks
- required follow-up
